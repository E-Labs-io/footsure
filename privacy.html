1. If we contacted you about Footsure

This section applies where we obtained your details from somewhere other than you — which is the case for everyone we have contacted so far. It is the information UK GDPR Article 14 requires us to give you.

What we hold, and where it came from

  • Your practice or business name, and the town it operates in.
  • A business email address — usually a role address such as info@ or office@, and in a few cases a named individual's work address.
  • The accounting software you publicly advertise working with.

All of it came from publicly available sources: your own website and public business directory listings. We did not buy a list, and we did not obtain anything from a data broker.

Why, and on what legal basis

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Our interest is in finding out, before building it, whether practices actually want a pay-per-statement statement converter with a balance-footing guarantee. We have written a legitimate interests assessment weighing that against your rights, and you can ask us for a copy of it.

We should be straight with you about one thing: we sent our first ten emails on 29 July 2026, before this notice existed. That was the wrong order, and this notice is us putting it right rather than pretending otherwise.

What we do not do

  • No open tracking, no click tracking, no tracking pixels. If you read our email, we do not know.
  • No profiling, no scoring, no automated decision-making about you.
  • No selling, renting or sharing your details with anyone for their own marketing.
  • No adding you to a drip sequence. One relevant email, and a reply if you write back.

2. If you asked us for early access

If you email us to register interest, we hold your email address, anything you choose to tell us (such as your practice name), and our correspondence. We use it to send you one email when Footsure launches, and to reply to you. The basis is legitimate interests — you asked us to get in touch. Ask us to stop at any time and we will.

We are not asking you to take that on trust in advance; we are telling you what has to be published before there is anything to trust.
